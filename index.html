About me

I am a senior robotics researcher at the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN), Indonesia, based at the Research Centre for Smart Mechatronics, where I lead the Robotics and System Intelligence (RoSI) research group. My work focuses on autonomous mobile robots, from mechanical design and perception to navigation and control.

I received my PhD from the Robot Intelligence Lab at Imperial College London, where I worked on autonomous mobile rescue robots and casualty extraction. My research brings together robot perception and model predictive control to help robots operate in complex environments. My current work centres on sensor fusion and localisation for autonomous ground vehicles and personal mobility.

Previously, I received a Master of Engineering Science from the University of New South Wales, Australia, and a Bachelor of Engineering from Gadjah Mada University, Indonesia.

I am General Chair of the 2nd International Conference on Smart Mechatronics (ICSMech 2026).

I am interested in designing, building, and making robots move. You can explore my research, read my publications, or find my code on GitHub.

Research interests

  • Autonomous mobile robotics
  • Sensor fusion and state estimation
  • Navigation and model predictive control
  • Robot perception and learning
  • Autonomous personal mobility
  • Rescue and inspection robotics
