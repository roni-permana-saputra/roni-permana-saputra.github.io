About

I am based at the Research Centre for Smart Mechatronics at BRIN. I received my PhD from the Robot Intelligence Lab at Imperial College London, where I worked on autonomous mobile rescue robots and casualty extraction. My research brings together robot perception and model predictive control to help robots operate in complex environments. My current work centres on sensor fusion and localisation for autonomous ground vehicles and personal mobility.

I am General Chair of the 2nd International Conference on Smart Mechatronics (ICSMech 2026).

Previously, I received a Master of Engineering Science from the University of New South Wales, Australia, and a Bachelor of Engineering from Gadjah Mada University, Indonesia.

I am interested in designing, building, and making robots move.

Autonomous personal mobility

Rescue robotics

Autonomous casualty extraction
