About me

I work on full-stack robotics: building robots that find their own way around, from the mechanical design to the software that drives them.

I am a senior researcher at the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN), Indonesia, where I lead the Robotics and System Intelligence (RoSI) research group at the Research Centre for Smart Mechatronics. Our robots range from single-passenger autonomous vehicles to delivery and inspection robots. My focus is the architecture and integration of the whole robot system, making sure that mechanical design, perception, navigation, and control come together as one robot that works reliably.

My current work centres on a practical question: how can a ground vehicle know where it is, reliably, using sensors that are affordable? I study sensor fusion and localisation for autonomous ground vehicles and personal mobility, and test the results on real platforms outdoors.

I received my PhD from the Robot Intelligence Lab at Imperial College London, where I developed ResQbot, a mobile rescue robot for casualty extraction, together with the perception and model predictive control methods it relies on. Before that, I received a Master of Engineering Science from the University of New South Wales, Australia, and a Bachelor of Engineering from Gadjah Mada University, Indonesia.

I am General Chair of the 2nd International Conference on Smart Mechatronics (ICSMech 2026).

I enjoy designing, building, and making robots move, and I am glad to hear from prospective collaborators and students. You can explore my research, read my publications, or find my code on GitHub.

Research interests

  • Autonomous mobile robotics
  • Sensor fusion and state estimation
  • Navigation and model predictive control
  • Robot perception and learning
  • Autonomous personal mobility
  • Rescue and inspection robotics
