About

I am based at the Research Centre for Smart Mechatronics at BRIN. I received my PhD from the Robot Intelligence Lab at Imperial College London, where I worked on autonomous mobile rescue robots and casualty extraction. My research brings together robot perception and model predictive control to help robots operate in complex environments.

Previously, I received a Master of Engineering Science from the University of New South Wales, Australia, and a Bachelor of Engineering from Gadjah Mada University, Indonesia.

I am interested in designing, building, and making robots move.
