# Roni Permana Saputra

Senior Researcher and Research Group Leader, Robotics and System Intelligence (RoSI)
Research Centre for Smart Mechatronics, National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN), Bandung, Indonesia

Website: https://roni-permana-saputra.github.io/

## About

I work on autonomous mobile robots, from mechanical design and perception to navigation and control. I received my PhD from the Robot Intelligence Lab at Imperial College London, where I worked on autonomous mobile rescue robots and casualty extraction.

## Research interests

- Autonomous mobile robotics
- Sensor fusion and state estimation
- Navigation and model predictive control
- Robot perception and learning
- Autonomous personal mobility
- Rescue and inspection robotics
